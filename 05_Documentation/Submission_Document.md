## Project description

WICKLIGHT – The Little Candle is a small 2D pixel-art platform game. The player is a little candle lost in a dark cave. In 60 seconds, the candle must collect five glowing embers, jump over spikes, avoid two flying moths and reach the exit door. The player has three lives. Every ember is placed close to a danger, so each reward needs a small risk.
